Course Overview

Built for ambitious medical students, tech professionals, and aspirants who need to command the English language with authority. This curriculum replaces textbook memorization with practical speech frameworks, voice modulation, and rapid-fluency triggers designed for boardrooms, clinical case presentations, and international interviews.

3-Month Curriculum Breakdown

Month 1: Foundations of Eloquence & Accent Neutralization

Weeks 1 – 4
  • •
    Week 1 (Days 1 & 2): Diagnosing hesitation blocks, psychological barriers, and vowel/consonant crispness.
  • •
    Week 2 (Days 3 & 4): Moving past native mental translation; eliminating fillers (*"um," "ah"*) through rhythm drills.
  • •
    Week 3 (Days 5 & 6): Upgrading basic vocabulary to high-impact workplace descriptors and small-talk dynamics.
  • •
    Week 4 (Days 7 & 8): Voice modulation, pitch control, pacing, and Monthly Milestone Voice Assessment.

Month 2: Professional, Academic & Clinical Articulation

Weeks 5 – 8
  • •
    Week 5 (Days 9 & 10): The "Bottom-Line First" communication framework; polite yet firm professional negotiation.
  • •
    Week 6 (Days 11 & 12): Clinical case briefings, technical summaries, and live mock project pitches.
  • •
    Week 7 (Days 13 & 14): Advanced corporate/academic phrasal verbs and dynamic Q&A handling under pressure.
  • •
    Week 8 (Days 15 & 16): Formal email/digital correspondence and Mid-Course Roundtable Simulation.

Month 3: Mastery, Interviews & Public Command

Weeks 9 – 12
  • •
    Week 9 (Days 17 & 18): STAR method mastery for job/college interviews and executive non-verbal body language.
  • •
    Week 10 (Days 19 & 20): Public speaking poise, conquering stage fright, and live peer debate sessions.
  • •
    Week 11 (Days 21 & 22): Crisis communication, handling difficult client pushbacks, and abstract thought leadership.
  • •
    Week 12 (Days 23 & 24): Final 1-on-1 viva voce simulation and Graduation Certificate Award Ceremony.
