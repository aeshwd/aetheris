Course Overview

Comprehensive regional anatomy of the upper limb designed specifically for medical students and aspirants. Master osteology, muscle attachments, critical nerve lesions, and clinical trauma correlations through high-yield 3D dissections and active clinical case correlations.

2-Month Modular Curriculum

Month 1: Osteology, Pectoral Region & Brachial Plexus

Weeks 1 – 4
  • •
    Week 1 (Days 1 & 2): Pectoral region fundamentals, clavipectoral fascia, and mammary gland anatomy.
  • •
    Week 2 (Days 3 & 4): Axilla boundaries, contents, and axillary artery branching patterns.
  • •
    Week 3 (Days 5 & 6): Shoulder complex, scapular muscle groups, and rotator cuff biomechanics.
  • •
    Week 4 (Days 7 & 8): Brachial plexus formation, root lesions (*Erb's & Klumpke's*), and arm compartments.

Month 2: Forearm, Hand, Clinical Traumas & OSCE Mock Viva

Weeks 5 – 8
  • •
    Week 5 (Days 9 & 10): Forearm flexor/extensor compartments, fascial spaces, and cubital fossa boundaries.
  • •
    Week 6 (Days 11 & 12): Hand intrinsic muscles, palm spaces, and critical nerve entrapments (*Carpal Tunnel Syndrome*).
  • •
    Week 7 (Days 13 & 14): Blood supply anastomoses, venous drainage, and high-yield clinical trauma correlations.
  • •
    Week 8 (Days 15 & 16): Comprehensive regional revision, OSCE case simulations, and Final Viva Voce Assessment.
